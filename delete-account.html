Delete your Resume Guide account

For users of the Resume Guide mobile app, published by Zenawi.

You can permanently delete your Resume Guide account and all the data associated with it at any time. You have two options:

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Resume Guide app on your phone.
  2. Tap the More tab at the bottom.
  3. Scroll to the Account section and tap Delete my account.
  4. Read the warning and confirm by tapping Delete forever.
  5. Re-enter your password (or sign in once more with Google) to prove the request is from you.
Your account and all data are removed within a few seconds. You'll be signed out and returned to the login screen.

Option 2 — Email us

If you no longer have the app installed, or you can't sign in:

Send an email to support@resumeguide.ai with the subject “Delete my account”.

Include:
  • The email address you used to sign in.
  • A short note confirming you want your account deleted.
We will delete your account within 7 days of receiving the request and confirm by email when it's done.
This action is permanent. Once deleted, your resumes, cover letters, saved job descriptions, candidate evaluations, owned premium templates, and remaining credits cannot be recovered.

What is deleted

The following is permanently removed from our servers:

Data typeWhat happens
Account (email, sign-in)Deleted immediately
Every resume you created or uploadedDeleted immediately
Cover letters and saved job descriptionsDeleted immediately
HR candidate evaluations and notesDeleted immediately
Favorites + owned premium templatesDeleted immediately
Usage counters and credit historyDeleted immediately

What is kept

We retain a small amount of information for legal and accounting purposes, even after the account is deleted:

Data typeRetention period
Purchase records (Google Play receipt IDs) Up to 7 years, as required by US tax law
Deletion audit log (anonymized — only a hashed id of the deleted account, no email, no name) Up to 2 years, for abuse-prevention forensics
Backups Backups are rotated and overwritten within 30 days of deletion

No retained record can be used to identify you personally. Refunds and tax reporting are handled through Google Play.

Questions?

Email support@resumeguide.ai and we'll respond within a few business days.
